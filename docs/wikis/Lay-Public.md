# Nexia-List, in Plain Language

**A place where your notes keep working on your behalf — and stay yours, forever.**

This page explains what Nexia-List is for people who don't write software — press, funders, decision-makers, and the simply curious. No technical background needed. If you want the developer overview instead, start at [Home](Home).

---

## The one-sentence version

Nexia-List is a note-taking tool that doesn't just *store* your thinking — it helps your notes find each other, reason about each other, and come back to you at the moment they matter, all on your own device, in files you own and can keep for a lifetime.

---

## A note is a letter to your future self

The idea at the heart of Nexia-List is simple: *a note is a letter we send to our future self.* You write something down today so a wiser, later you can read it. Everything the tool does is in service of making that letter arrive — and making the self who reads it better for every letter before it.

## What "management of mind" means

Most note apps are very good at the *first* half of thinking and quietly give up on the second:

- **Capturing** a thought — apps do this well.
- **Connecting** it to related thoughts — apps do this passably.
- **Reasoning** over what you've written — *"why do I believe this? what does it depend on?"* — most apps don't do this at all.
- **Resurfacing** the right old note at the right moment, without you going to dig for it — most apps leave your archive as a graveyard you have to excavate.

Nexia-List treats that neglected second half — **reasoning** and **resurfacing** — as the whole point. Your archive stops being a filing cabinet and becomes a correspondent that writes back: it volunteers a forgotten note that's suddenly relevant, and it lets you lay out an argument and see how confident each conclusion really is.

## Why it stays yours

This is the part that makes Nexia-List different from most modern tools:

| Promise | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Local-first** | Your notes live on your device, not on someone's server. |
| **You own your data** | Notes are saved as plain, human-readable files you can open, keep, and move anywhere. No proprietary format. |
| **No cloud, no account, no lock-in** | Nothing to sign up for, nothing to leave the device, nothing to unsubscribe from. |
| **Runs after the company is gone** | Because it runs on your machine from files you hold, it keeps working even if the project stops tomorrow. |
| **Works offline, everywhere** | It runs in an ordinary web browser, online or off, on whatever device you have. |

You are never renting access to your own thinking.

## Where it honestly stands

Nexia-List is **early software** — about a third of the way to its first full milestone (roughly 35% of the way to "MVP"). The foundations work: you can create, link, and search notes today. The recall and reasoning features described above are designed in detail and being built, not yet shipped. We'd rather say that plainly than oversell it. It is open source, free, and licensed so anyone can build on it (MPL-2.0 for the code, CC-BY-SA-4.0 for the documentation).
